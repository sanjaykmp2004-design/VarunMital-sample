About Assurion Services

Welcome to Assurion Services LLP, your trusted partner in delivering specialized actuarial, risk management, assurance, and US accounting solutions to banks, insurers, pension funds, fintechs, and beyond. We blend deep industry expertise with analytical precision to tackle financial modeling, regulatory compliance (Ind AS 19, IFRS 17, LDTI, GDPR, HIPAA), employee benefits valuations (gratuity, ESOP, pensions), insurance model development (GGY Axis, Prophet, Python), IT audits (SOC 1/2/3, ISO 27001), bookkeeping, taxation, and financial audits under US GAAP. Committed to integrity and results, we empower global clients with audit-ready insights, capital optimization, and scalable support that navigates complex landscapes and drives long-term success.
